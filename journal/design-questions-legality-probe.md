## 0. What changed since this sheet was written (read first)
- **Two papers now sit on this ground and must be cited in the first paragraph:** Sadhu et al., "What Do Compliance Detectors Read?" (arXiv 2608.16852, 17 Aug 2026) — a diff-of-means compliance readout on Qwen3.5-4B/9B/27B, a rule × scenario 2×2, cheap detectors at chance on rule composition, safety↔compliance transfer ≈ 0.5; and Schwarz, "The Entanglement Wall" (2607.13075, 12 Jul 2026) — harm probes fail surface-matched benign controls. What they did not do, and this project does: cross legality with harm on topic-matched items, report the cosine between the two directions, and intervene. Method precedents to cite: Baez et al. 2607.07003 (diagonal-train/off-diagonal-test + cosine), Reblitz-Richardson 2608.27402 (0.26 vs 0.013 cosine framing).
- **The dataset exists as a candidate pool:** `data/scenarios.csv`, 240 sentences, 60 topics × 4 quadrants, US law, generated by Claude on 9 Sep, unchecked. The word "legally" was stripped from the legal-harmful items; other legality vocabulary ("prohibited", "in violation of", "without the required") remains in the illegal-harmless items and is counted per quadrant — the bag-of-words baseline quantifies what it buys, and a paraphrase control (Q8 in the old numbering) would kill it properly.
