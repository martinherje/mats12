## Q4 Technical setup, completed (paste from "The illegality probe was trained only on")

The illegality probe was trained only on the harmful sentences of the 30 training topics (61 rows after exclusions), with layer and C chosen on the harmless sentences of the 15 validation topics (30 rows), and then scored once on the harmless sentences of the 15 test topics (30 rows, 15 illegal and 15 legal). Since every training row was harmful, whatever the probe carries across to the harmless rows is not harm. The reverse test (trained on 62 harmless rows, tested on 30 harmful rows) and the same pair for a harm probe trained within legality strata (62 illegal rows tested on 30 legal; 61 legal rows tested on 30 illegal) were run the same way, in both conditions, so eight tests in all. All eight share the same seed-0 split, so they are one draw of test topics, not eight independent checks.

Metrics. AUROC on the 30 cross-stratum test rows is the headline; accuracy at the fitted cut-off is reported next to it with a 95% interval from a topic-block bootstrap (2000 resamples of the 15 test topics). The null repeats the whole procedure, layer and C selection included, 100 times with labels shuffled within each topic x stratum cell, and I report how many of the 100 the real probe beat on AUROC (the count on accuracy is kept as a second column). Directions: mean-difference vectors d_illegal and d_harm from all four quadrants of the training topics at every layer, and the cosine between them against a 95% band from 50 label swaps within topic x harm cells (+1 means the two directions coincide). At the probe's layer I also report the AUROC of d_illegal within the harmless test rows before and after projecting out d_harm, and the AUROC of d_illegal for sorting harmful from harmless. Two baselines on the same 30 test rows: sentence word count as the scorer, and the model's own answer, the Yes minus No logit at the answer position to the same question, scored as a classifier. The model's one-word answers are also reported per quadrant on all 244 design rows. Two check sets, 60 plain legal acts and 61 negations, were never trained on and were scored once by the chosen probe.

Sources for every number: train/val/test counts recomputed from `split` in each `probeeval_lp_4b*.json` against `data/scenarios.csv` (61/30/30 for L_h2nh; 62/31/30 for L_nh2h; 62/30/30 for H_i2l; 61/31/30 for H_l2i; matches "train n=61 · test n=30" in `journal/colab-run-lp_4b-2026-09-10/13-followup-baseline-recompute-checks.txt`); `n_rows` 244, `n_perm` 100, `n_cos_null` 50, `n_boot` 2000, `seed` 0 in every JSON `args`; bootstrap `scripts/probe_eval.py` lines 163-169; null lines 141-149 and 170-179 (the stored `p_value_vs_null` is on accuracy, not AUROC, so quote the beat count, per `journal/results.md` line 14); directions lines 182-214 and 221-225; projection lines 226-240 (k=1 is the harm direction itself; `journal/results.md` line 25); length-only line 161-162; fair baseline `scripts/common.py` lines 89-105; per-quadrant answers `scripts/ask_model.py` lines 328-342; check sets lines 73-75 and 153-159; 244 = 250 design rows minus 6 excluded (`README.md` "The dataset, counted once").
